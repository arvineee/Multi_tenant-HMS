Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

This policy explains what data MediCore HMIS collects, how it's used, and who's responsible for it. If you're a patient, your hospital or clinic — not MediCore HMIS directly — is who you should contact about your own medical records; see "Who controls your data" below for why.

1. Who this covers

MediCore HMIS is software that hospitals, clinics, and dispensaries in Kenya use to run their day-to-day operations — outpatient and inpatient care, pharmacy, billing, lab and radiology ordering. This policy applies to every organization using the platform and everyone whose data passes through it: facility staff with login accounts, and the patients those facilities treat.

2. Who controls your data

Each hospital or clinic that signs up is a data controller for its own patients' information under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 — they decide what's collected and why, and they're who you should approach with questions about your own medical record, or to exercise your rights under that Act. MediCore HMIS acts as a data processor: we provide the system the facility uses to store and manage that data, on their instructions, and we don't sell it, share it with other organizations on the platform, or use it for anything beyond running the service.

3. What we collect

Depending on how a given account is used, the platform holds:

  • Organization & facility data — hospital name, address, county, contact details, facility level.
  • Staff account data — name, username, email, phone, role, and login activity for anyone with an account.
  • Patient data, entered by facility staff — demographics, contact and next-of-kin details, vitals, diagnoses, prescriptions, lab and radiology orders/results, admission and ward records, and billing/insurance information.
  • Payment data for subscription billing — processed through IntaSend (M-Pesa); MediCore HMIS does not itself store your M-Pesa PIN or full card details.

4. How it's used

  • To provide the clinical, pharmacy, billing, and administrative functions each facility uses.
  • To keep each organization's data walled off from every other organization on the platform — enforced at the database query level, not just hidden in the interface.
  • To secure accounts — password hashing, login lockout after repeated failed attempts, and role-based access so each staff member only sees what their job requires.
  • To maintain an audit trail of who did what and when, viewable by each facility's own administrators.
  • To process subscription payments and communicate about your account.

We do not use patient or facility data for advertising, and we do not sell data to third parties.

5. Data security

Measures already built into the platform include:

  • Passwords stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • Account lockout after repeated failed login attempts.
  • Role-based access control, down to a dedicated system-maintenance tier that itself has no self-service way to be granted — reducing the number of people who could ever see everything.
  • A full audit log of account and data changes.
  • Organization-level data isolation enforced in every query, not just in what the interface shows.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — but these aren't afterthoughts bolted on for this policy; they're load-bearing parts of how the platform is built.

6. Data retention

Patient medical records are retained according to each facility's own record-keeping obligations under Kenyan health regulations — that's a decision for the facility as data controller, not MediCore HMIS. Account and audit-log data is retained for as long as an organization's account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal and accounting purposes.

7. Your rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

If a patient's data is held by a facility using this platform, they have the right to:

  • Be informed of how their data is used.
  • Access the personal data held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Object to processing, and request deletion, subject to the facility's legal obligation to keep certain medical records.
  • Lodge a complaint with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

These requests should go to the facility that treated you, as the data controller. Facility staff with account questions can contact us directly (see below).

8. Cookies & hosting

The platform uses a session cookie strictly to keep you logged in — no third-party advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts and some interface styling are loaded from Google Fonts and a CDN; these services may log the request the same way any web request does, but no patient data is sent to them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify organization administrators directly.
