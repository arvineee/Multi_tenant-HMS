Refund Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

Free trial

Every new organization gets a free trial to explore MediCore HMIS before paying anything — no card required to start it. Use this period to confirm the platform fits your facility before subscribing.

Monthly subscriptions

Monthly subscription fees are charged for the upcoming billing period. Because access is granted immediately for that period, payments already made for the current month are non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges — you'll keep access through the end of the period you've already paid for.

One-time (outright) purchase

A one-time purchase is a larger, considered commitment rather than a recurring charge. If you believe the platform genuinely doesn't work for your facility, contact us within 7 days of purchase to discuss a refund — this is handled case by case rather than as an automatic right, since it follows significant setup on our side.

Billing errors

If you're charged in error — a duplicate charge, or a charge after you'd already cancelled — contact us and we'll correct it.

How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp at +{{ support_whatsapp_number }} with your organization name and the payment in question.

These are starting terms, not yet confirmed business policy — if your actual intended refund terms differ (a specific refund window, prorated refunds, etc.), this page should be updated to match before it's relied on by customers.
